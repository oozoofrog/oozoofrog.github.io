# Blog editorial principles

Lead the home page with the most recent public post. Readers should see its title and summary before they scroll on a phone or desktop. Use the author's title and summary when listing a post.

Use generated illustrations to support the writing. Keep them outside the reading path. Readers must be able to find and read each post with images turned off. Keep charts, screenshots, and other evidence with the text they explain.

Keep the author introduction on the About page. Use the home page and the archive to help readers choose a post.
