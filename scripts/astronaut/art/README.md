# Astronaut frog turnaround

Use the original lunar painting for the seated gesture and the upward gaze. The four-view sheet supplies inferred side and rear designs. Each panel describes the same frog at rest. The sheet is a build input for the character atlas.

## Design and motion

- Keep the pupils, closed mouth seam and helmet readable in silhouette. Fit the hands to the jaw and keep the boot soles in contact with the ground.
- Paint in surface coordinates. Let the model, its normals and its outline geometry determine what the camera sees.
- Stage the resting pose first. Keep the head, hands and feet planted during the blink. Roll the upper lids down and lift the lower lids to meet them. Paint the lids in the closed pose. Hold contact briefly and reopen more slowly. Delay one lid slightly.
- Ease a button-driven camera turn along the orbit. Follow a drag directly. Settle immediately when reduced motion or the motion switch disables animation.

The motion choices follow the treatment of staging, timing, arcs and slow in and out in John Lasseter’s *Principles of Traditional Animation Applied to 3D Computer Animation* (SIGGRAPH 1987).

Use the attached painting as the identity and material reference. Make a production character turnaround painting sheet, landscape, four separate seated full-body figures of the SAME astronaut frog in one row on plain warm ivory paper, all at identical scale, equal spacing, complete uncut feet and domes. From left to right: true straight-on anatomical front, left profile facing left, exact back, right profile facing right. Orthographic camera, same low seated pose seen in source: hips on floor, bent knees raised slightly and both heels forward, elbows resting on knees, small green hands cupping cheeks, gentle upward gaze. Wide green head, big oval cream eyes and solid dark hand-inked pupils, subdued curved mouth dividing green muzzle from cream throat, no grin, clear round helmet, ivory wrinkled pressure suit, soft chunky boots and graphite soles. In back view the backpack is a compact rounded-rectangular cream life-support case with orange edge piping, gray latches, one horizontal vent panel, two short hoses over both shoulders. Thick directional oil-gouache impasto, broken dark brush-pen silhouettes, small ochre, faded cobalt and gray accents faithfully match the reference painting. Emphasize readable weight and clear silhouettes. All four are drawings of a single unchanging design, no Earth, stars, rocks, lettering, grid lines, shadows beyond small contact shadows, labels, watermarks, perspective foreshortening or glossy CG lighting.
